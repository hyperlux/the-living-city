## Key Additions Needed

### 1. Metabolic Citizenship (Gap 1)

> "You own part of the city's metabolism. Not metaphorically—actually. The solar facades that power your building pay dividends to your account. The biogas plant processing neighborhood organic waste sends you a quarterly check."

**Models to reference:**
- Alaska Permanent Fund ($1,600/year per resident)
- Denmark wind cooperatives (86% cooperative-owned, 100,000+ families)
- Singapore public housing (80% ownership pathway)

### 2. The Good Attractors Framework (Gap 2)

The 10 properties that distinguish self-sustaining systems:
1. Minimal Core (≤10 principles)
2. Hierarchical (clear precedence)
3. Generative (destination, not route)
4. Self-reinforcing (practice increases commitment)
5. Wide basin (many starting points)
6. Robust (self-corrects)
7. Scale-invariant (works at 10x and 0.1x)
8. Interpretable (agents can articulate membership)
9. Transmissible (survives translation)
10. Evolvable (adapts without losing identity)

### 3. NIMBY → YIMBY Transformation (Gap 4)

> "Opposition isn't irrational. It's a rational response to extractive infrastructure design. The question is not 'how do we overcome opposition?' but 'why is there opposition to overcome?'"

The solution: Restructure incentives so hosting infrastructure = receiving dividends.
